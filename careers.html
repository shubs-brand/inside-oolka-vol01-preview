How we build

AI isn't a feature. It's how we build.

India doesn't need another AI app. It needs a financial intelligence layer that can read a credit report, spot what's wrong, and act on it. We're building that layer.

  • It acts. It doesn't advise. The agent reads the report, drafts the dispute, talks to the lender, and gets the outcome. A to-do list is a failure state.
  • Sub-second on 2G. A village, 11 PM, a weak signal. They get an answer fast, or they give up. We build so the second case never happens.
  • Every language, natively. Not an English product translated down. Twenty-two scheduled languages, built in from day one.
  • Real money, no drama. Inference that bends under load instead of breaking, because the failure mode here is someone's loan.

How we think and work

Six things we believe, and what they look like on a Tuesday.

  • 01Do the work. Advice is a failure state.The agent drafts the dispute and follows up until it's done. So do we.
  • 02Care who is on the other end.Every number in the dashboard is a person who got told no. We decide like it's our own family waiting on the answer.
  • 03Default to trust.People hand us the most private part of their lives. We earn it by being honest, clear, and in their corner.
  • 04Build for Bharat first.82.6% of our users live beyond the metros. The product has to work for them before it works for anyone.
  • 05Keep the team small. Do more with less.About twenty engineers carrying eight million people. Fewer people, more ownership, better work.
  • 06Ship in weeks, not quarters.You'll own real calls and watch them land with users while you can still remember making them.

The one question we ask about every hire: would we hand this person our own family's credit report?
