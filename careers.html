India has 1.4 billion people. Yet getting a loan, fixing a credit score, or simply understanding what is happening with your own money still needs expertise most people don't have.

The system was designed for the few who could already decode it. Everyone else has been left to guess, and to pay for guessing wrong. That is the problem we exist to solve. Not an app feature. A country that can read its own credit.

AI ISN'T A FEATURE.

India doesn't need another AI app. It needs a financial intelligence layer that can read a credit report, spot what's wrong, and act on it. We're building that layer.

// agents, not chatbots

It acts, it doesn't advise

Our AI reads the report, drafts the dispute, talks to the lender, and gets the outcome. A to-do list is a failure state.

// latency

Sub-second on 2G

A user in a village opens the app at 11 PM on a weak connection. They get an answer fast, or they give up. We build for the second case never happening.

// bharat

Every language, natively

Not an English product translated down. Built so it thinks in the language the user thinks in.

// stakes

Real money, no drama

Inference that bends under load instead of breaking. Fallbacks and circuit breakers, because the failure mode here is someone's loan.

How we build

HOW WE ACTUALLY WORK.

01

Do the work

Advice is easy. We act. We read the report, draft the email, talk to the bank, and don't stop until it's done. We build the same way.

02

Care who's on the other end

Every number in the dashboard is a person who got told no. We decide like it's our own family waiting on the answer.

03

Default to trust

People hand us the most private part of their lives. We earn it by being honest, clear, and in their corner. Always.

04

Build for Bharat

Built from scratch for a shopkeeper in Siwan and a van owner in Gurugram, in the words they think in.

05

Small team, real ownership

Twenty-odd people carrying eight million. You'll own real calls and watch them land in weeks, not quarters.

The one question we ask about every hire: would we hand this person our own family\'s credit report?
